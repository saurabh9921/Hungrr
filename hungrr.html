Our Story

Born in Kagal,
Built for Kagal

Hungrr was born from a simple belief — that the incredible food Kagal's local kitchens and street vendors make deserves to reach more people, more conveniently.

We bridge the gap between hungry customers and their favourite local eateries. When an order comes in via WhatsApp, our dedicated delivery partner picks it up and delivers it fresh — no complicated apps, no hidden fees, no fuss.

We are a community-first platform proudly rooted in Kagal. Every order you place directly supports local businesses and creates livelihoods for our delivery team.
